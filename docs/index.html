Two games, one adventure

OoTCraft runs Minecraft: Java Edition 1.21 and Ship of Harkinian (the community PC port of Ocarina of Time) side by side, connected live.

🧱 Minecraft is you

  • Movement, camera, health and hunger
  • Inventory, crafting, every 1.21 block and recipe
  • Minecraft's hand and HUD drawn over Hyrule

🗡️ Zelda is the world

  • Hyrule, enemies, bosses and NPCs
  • Dungeons, cutscenes and the whole story
  • Press F5 to become Link, with his own controls
⛏️

Dig into Hyrule

Mine Zelda's ground block by block. Underneath is a real 1.21 world: ores, caves, dungeons, ancient cities, down to bedrock.

🏗️

Build anywhere

Every Minecraft block, with its real textures and 3D model. Link can stand on it and hookshot to wood.

🔥

TNT and fire

Blow craters into Hyrule, or set Zelda's grass alight with flint and steel.

⚔️

Fight like Steve

Minecraft weapons land as real Zelda attacks, so enemies with weak points still work.

🎒

Zelda items in your hotbar

Bombs, hookshot and the rest of Link's items become Minecraft items you can use.

🧗

Climb, crawl, swim

Vines and ladders climb like Minecraft ladders, and Zelda's crawlspaces use Minecraft's crawl pose.

🧍

You are Steve

Link is drawn as Steve with your own skin, in the world and on the pause screen.

💬

The story still works

Talk, open chests and watch cutscenes. Zelda takes over when needed, then hands control back.

Install in four steps

The installer builds everything on your own PC from official sources. You bring the games.

🪟 Windows 10/11🎮 Your own Ocarina of Time ROM🟩 Your own Minecraft: Java Edition💾 ~15 GB free
  1. Download OoTCraft from GitHub and extract it.
  2. Double-click Install-OoTCraft.bat. It installs the build tools, builds Ship of Harkinian with OoTCraft on your PC, and adds an OoTCraft profile to your own Minecraft Launcher.
  3. Start OoTCraft from your desktop. The first time, choose your Ocarina of Time ROM.
  4. Load a save. Your Minecraft Launcher opens: pick OoTCraft and press Play. Minecraft tucks itself away and you're Steve in Hyrule.
