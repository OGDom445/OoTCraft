# Code of Conduct

OoTCraft is a friendly, welcoming community for anyone who loves Zelda, Minecraft and modding, at any skill level.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people asking for help.
- Give constructive feedback on code and ideas, not on people.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sharing, requesting or linking ROMs, cracked games, or other pirated material.
- Spam, advertising unrelated projects, or impersonating others.
- Posting other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests, and may warn, mute or ban people who
break these rules, on GitHub and on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/w5sxzdPtT7). To report a problem, contact a
maintainer privately on the Discord.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
